committed/selected generation. On inspected native ed1afb082eb3e46cf2edae9d8f68fb67b09ee4df,

### Fixed capture interface and uncertainty

Implement a fixed trusted gateway entry function, before listener creation, that
retains its partial startup owner before importing/observing native bootstrap.
Its internal capture method takes no caller-supplied generation, qualified flag,
module map or sys.path replacement. It retains the actual process bootstrap/module
objects and their entry origins, the actual activated import roots and loaded
third-party module origins, matched against independently admitted roots/inventories.
It must join those same objects/values after filesystem/import/currentness effects.
Capture is not possession or reconstruction of the original PM lease receipt.
Multiple/unknown activation roots, module origins outside admitted roots, late
capture without original startup continuity, failed observation or changed objects
latch uncertainty and cannot be repaired by recapturing inside a request.
The native gateway continues through its normal entry; no source monkeypatch,
bootstrap reload or new environment selection is an activation-capture mechanism.
A retained startup owner may allocate separate operation/channel owners; each
must retain its own exact input/handshake/task and cannot borrow a sibling's facts.

## PS2 — fixed broker startup owner

Use an independently supervised isolated broker entry, independent approved
runtime/source/parser inventories, manager/reaper binding and fixed exec-only
worker recipe. Retain partial role owner before acquisition. Load only its broker
key; close all original key descriptors before creating any worker. No seed,
signer, key FD or signing callable is delegated to the worker. Trusted exec-launch
internals are not blanket-prohibited: there must be no untrusted pre-exec execution
and no retained role key material or signing capability in the worker after exec.
The gateway does not become the child reaper, and Kanban remains enabled.
The original service owner and registry retain exact child/wait/control/pipe and
termination/drain evidence; EOF, task completion or a signed statement alone
cannot replace those facts. Runtime/root changes retire this owner permanently;
there is no in-request inventory regeneration, key rotation or owner replacement.

## PS3 — one original channel/handshake binding

Each role factory binds its exact original role/key owner, listener/service boot,
installation generation, operator pins, qualified clock-domain and channel. Retain
partial channel/handshake acquisitions before native/crypto effects. No valid
role/operation owner may be borrowed by another instance, role, generation,
listener or process. Per-operation handshake/task/channel facts are not transferable
to another allocation; this does not prohibit one retained startup root from
owning multiple separately isolated operation/channel owners. The existing handshake/phase owners perform only their
fixed operations; they expose no arbitrary sign/digest-approval API.

Revocation retires future network/publication authority immediately but preserves
original local resource/descriptor/task/child cleanup ownership. An unknown close
is not retried against a reused descriptor. No new allowance is minted for
reconnect, cleanup, role rotation or an ambiguous final acknowledgement.

## PS4 — normal native acceptance and ACK join

1. On the original work task, obtain current_correlation from the actual retained
   normal receiver. Rejoin original fact/frame/count/canonical terminal data and

2. Validate original authenticated peer/boot/domain/generation and admitted broker
   producer contract, original input/profile/kind/deadline/challenge/epoch/source
   and RESULT-body digest. Retain actual native acceptance intent before effects.
   Correlation DATA or a signature alone cannot mint terminal custody.
3. Join actual native read-worker DRAIN and the original native operation
   completion under remaining original work time. PresentationNativeService.drain
   joins its actual native read task/thread only; it proves neither broker reaping
   nor broker terminal/resource closure. Retain those separate typed completion
   obligations, never project invented child events into the native JobOwner. Keep broker closure and local gateway closure
   separate. Do not fabricate JobOwner child events or reuse a different reader.
4. Derive exactly one signed ACK for the exact frozen terminal envelope digest,
   on the original channel/controller lifecycle and retained sequence. Retain
   counted write/partial-write and original control cursor read evidence. A lost
   or partial ACK leaves debt; do not resend or reinterpret EOF as acceptance.
5. Broker ACK acceptance may retire only its original terminal journal after local
   closure. It cannot release gateway headers, native policy or job credits.
   Native credit release requires its separate original completion proof.
6. Only after all required custody joins and fresh PRE_EMIT may the authenticated
   publisher adopt/emit the original image candidate. No intermediate result,
   acceptance intent or successful ACK count publishes bytes. On ambiguity,
   withdraw the original candidate, preserve debt and refuse without fallback.

Cleanup/ABORTED/direct/earlier terminal positions retain their separate original
reader/cause/minimum-budget contracts; normal completion must not be generalized
by resetting a cursor or treating a cleanup stamp as a new allowance.
